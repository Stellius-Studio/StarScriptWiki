## Description
Pushes updated advancement, tag, and recipe data to every connected client — broader than
`updateserverrecipes`, useful right after registering/removing advancements or recipes at
runtime.


## Attributes
None.


## Examples
```yaml
Skills:
- loadadvancement{key="myplugin:custom/first_kill";json="..."}
- updateserverresources
```


## Aliases
None.
